WHO WE ARE

We are a Christ-centered Christian church with commitment to the sole authority of scriptures and the leading of the Holy Spirit, with a mission to bring Christ to the hearts of fallen, broken and hurting people.

OUR VISION

Is to continue to be a Christ-centered church which provides people with the types of fellowship, worship, teaching, empowerment and Christian support that will help them develop into the kind of individuals God desires them to be.

OUR MISSION

Seeks to help people discover meaning, purpose and direction for their lives; to help them find answers to life's perplexing questions, and ultimately lead them to the saving knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.

OUR CORE BELIEFS

Although there will always be doctrinal beliefs over which Christians differ, we believe there are fundamental doctrines which define and uphold core beliefs of the Christian faith.

We believe the Bible, consisting of both the Old Testament and New Testament scriptures, to be the divinely revealed and inspired word of God. We believe that these scriptures are the communicated truths of God to us and therefore are authoritative rules of faith and conduct for human beings.

We believe that there is one God and that he is the creator of all things visible and invisible; who is eternally self-existent, omniscient, immutable, omnipresent, and omnipotent.

We believe in the Lord Jesus Christ, the Eternal and Only Begotten Son of God, who was born of the Virgin Mary; who in his humanity lived in perfect obedience to the Father and voluntarily gave his life in death to atone for the sins of humanity as the perfect substitute - Lamb of God. We believed that he rose from the dead on the third day and ascended into heaven...

We believe that the Holy Spirit came forth from the Father to convict the world of sin, to regenerate, sanctify, indwell, empower, teach, lead and comfort those who believe and accept the Lord Jesus Christ as their personal Saviour.

We believe that humankind was created in the image of God and sinless, but fell through a voluntary act of disobedience and became sinful and depraved. As a result of sin humankind became separated from God and is unable to, by our own works or intelligence, change our sinful condition.

We believe the Sabbath, which preceded the nation and laws of Israel, to be God's model and intent for his people to take a meaningful break from their regular activities to offer sacrifices of praise, celebrate, worship, fellowship, and become spiritually renewed as they build their relationship with him and their fellowship with fellow believers.

Ministry

SHEKINAH MEN'S

The men's ministry serves a vital and necessary function in our local church. It also serves as a mechanism through which men are empowered to pursue excellence in all that they do and to become all that God intended them to be in the families, careers, leadership roles, and ministries.

The ministry is not limited to men in our local body but seeks to reach out to the wider community of men to bring meaning, purpose, direction, hope, and wholeness in Christ.

OUR VISION

Our vision for our men's ministry is to become more and more effective as we empower and help men attain a high level of effectiveness and meaning in their lives. We aspire to be effective in facilitating growth and change in the lives of men in areas of spiritual, social, personal, emotional, relational, and intellectual.

OUR MISSION

As a ministry, our primary purpose is to minister to the whole person, with the aim of leading men to a saving knowledge of Christ and facilitating growth and development in those called to the Kingdom of Christ. All activities are based on the objective of bringing the unbeliever to Jesus Christ, by encouraging and developing active participation of men in the ministries of the church.

GROWTH AND DEVELOPMENT

The men's ministry is made up of men of all ages united by the following objectives and goals:

To provide effective Christian leadership
To inspire, teach and equip men to become involved in lifestyle evangelism
To create opportunities for men to serve by challenging, training and involving them in their gifted ministries under the leadership of the Pastor(s).
To challenge men to organize and support activities for younger men
To challenge men to sponsor and participate in the growth of the local church

Women's Ministry

WOMEN OF ESSENCE

As the Women of Essence, our goal is to bring hope and meaning to women everywhere, to empower women through workshops, group sessions and other interactive methods, to plan church and community activities where women can experience meaningful fellowship, joy and friendship that comes from being loved by others.

OUR VISION

To continue to expand the boundaries of our Women's Ministry beyond the limits of our local church into our wider community, bringing empowerment, hope and healing to women in their various conditions of life.

OUR MISSION

The mission of the Women of Essence Ministry is to impact the lives of other women by empowering them toward purpose and wholeness. We strive to encourage and facilitate wholeness in areas of needs such as, emotional, intellectual, spiritual, relations and physical. Our mission is to make a difference in the lives of young adult women, divorced women, single mothers, married women, widows, shut-ins, career women as well as those who are lonely, abused and depressed. Our ultimate goal is to lead those who are hopeless and unsaved to the saving knowledge of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.

GROWTH AND DEVELOPMENT

The women's ministry also strives to engage in Women fellowship conference yearly, Women's annual retreat, monthly group sessions, Mentoring programs, Mother daughter gala, banquets, and one and one interaction to encourage the growth of individuals health and wellness need.

Children's Ministry

MIGHTY WARRIORS FOR CHRIST

More and more churches are now recognizing the importance and influence of children ministry in the lives of children. In fact, children ministry is now considered by many to be amongst the fore front of evangelism and discipleship in the 21st century church.

Given the various forms of immorality and inappropriate influences our children are exposed to, a Christian ministry that provides innovative and effective ways to reach, nurture and foster responsible and godly characters is without doubt necessary and needed.

OUR VISION

Is to empower children into maturity. We strive to provide the kind of environment and opportunities which will help cultivate strong, purposeful, godly and responsible characters in our children, which will lead them to become devoted Christians and good citizens of society.

OUR MISSION

Our mission is to teach our children Godly principles through music and arts, reading, drama, and various outdoor activities.

We provide the environment and tools through which our children can be built up socially, spiritually, intellectually, emotionally, physically and personally.

THE MIGHTY WARRIORS FOR CHRIST

The Mighty Warriors for Christ is the name of our dynamic and growing Children's Ministry. Although there is a great emphasis placed on spiritual development in our children, given the scope of the needs of our children, our ministry is by no means limited to the spiritual. We believe that children must be empowered not only to take their place in the Body of Christ, but also as responsible citizens in the wider society.

In view of this, our children's ministry is committed to encouraging the development of a wide range of skills in the children. This includes, but is not limited to, intellectual, social, relational, leadership, communication, and creative skills.

OUR GOALS:

Social Skills development

We work to achieve this through planned and spontaneous social experiences. We develop group activities that will involve them to listen to others' thoughts and generate ideas together as well as taking them on outings, camps and field trips to encourage them to communicate on a casual level and share their learning experiences with one another.

Personal Skills Development

We strive to achieve this by providing opportunities for children to give presentations and encourage them to praise one another. An environment is created which encourage and foster a sense of belonging and safety in our children. At the end of each year children are presented with certificates for outstanding achievements in areas such as "big helper" or any areas they have made improvements.

Spiritual Awareness and Development

We strive to encourage the awareness of God and his moral attributes in the mind of our children. To achieve this we engage their thinking and discussion about stories of the Bible which highlight the characters of God as loving, patient, and caring. Particular activities are also used to help children apply their understanding of spirituality. These include but not limited to discussion, presentations, arts and crafts, songs, skits, role-play, and more.

Physical Skills Development

While young, children generally have a lot of energy and enthusiasm for the outdoors. We aim to provide the kind of environment and opportunities where children can learn various skills while having fun at the same time. During these activities we include children from the wider community. Here we encourage our children to invite their friends to join the crew for fun and learning.

Emotional

Emotions are very important to the development of a child. We strive to provide the teaching and activities through which children can begin to recognize and use their emotions in creative and productive ways. It is also our goal to help children understand that others' have feelings through activities and conflict resolutions.

Intellectual Skills Development

Every experience can be a learning experience. We strive to create themes and various activities and information associated with those themes that will help the children grasp and learn new concepts and ideas as well as their applications.

HOSPITALITY MINISTRY

The Hospitality Ministry provides a warm, nurturing environment for our members and guests to create experiences that reflects the love of God.

Our hospitality team will welcome you into our warm and friendly environment. Our team is passionate about displaying God's love through service. "Be hospitable to one another without grumbling. As each one received a gift, minister it to one another, as good stewards of the manifold grace of God." 1 peter 4:9-10.

Our ministry includes Ushers, Greeters, Parking attendants and a Culinary team. Fellowship meal is important to us, therefore, our Culinary team coordinates food services to align with the overall goals of the church throughout the year.

PRAYER MINISTRY

The central purpose of this ministry is to support all areas of the church in terms of its initiatives, plans, vision, leadership, growth as well as its physical and spiritual health. We are aware that the devil exists to try and defeat the people of God and the plans of the church. Our prayer mandate is to fight, prevent and defeat him through prayer and the word so the church can fulfil its God-given mandate. The ongoing victory of the church moves beyond what we know and can do to who we know and what He can do. We reach Him through prayer. Through prayer we, through Christ, break chains, undo heavy burdens, and set captives free.

The prayer team serves the church as altar workers and provides support and encouragement when needed. One of our tasks is to visit those who are sick, praying with them and encouraging them with the word.

The prayer ministry has established itself as a vital component to the church's existence, and we are endeavoring to keep ourselves aligned to the task that we are called to do.
